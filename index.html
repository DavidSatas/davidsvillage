Welcome to David's Village

In 1989, a being called David Satas built a village in a snow-covered realm between the layers of our reality. The night is eternal, existential music hangs quietly in the air, and the snow is generally warm and comforting.

The village was made as a safe place for all sentient beings to learn about David's beliefs.
