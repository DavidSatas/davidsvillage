Welcome to David’s Village!

In 1989, a being called David Satas built a village. It exists in a snow covered realm located in between the layers of our reality. The night there is eternal, and existential music plays very subtly in the atmosphere. It’s tundra like environment has snow that’s generally very warm and comforting.

The theme of the village’s architecture and ambience represents the best of these cultures:

  • Classical European
  • Italian, Jewish, and African American
  • East Asian.

And on top of all that a consistent Christmas and Autumn aesthetic

The village was made as a safe place for all sentient beings to learn about David’s beliefs. Go explore the village and don’t forget to stop by the library to read his manifesto!
